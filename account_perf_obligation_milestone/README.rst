This module adds milestones to performance obligations using the daily
pro-rata recognition method.

A milestone is a date and a cumulative amount: the amount that must be
recognized at the end of that date. The obligation is recognized
linearly up to the amount of the first milestone, then linearly from one
milestone to the next, and finally linearly from the last milestone up
to the total amount at the end of the recognition period.

Milestone amounts must have the same sign as the total amount and cannot
exceed it. They may decrease from one milestone to the next (income can
be de-recognized). Milestones dated before the start date, or on or
after the end date (where the total amount is recognized), are ignored.

Usage
=====

1. Create a performance obligation with the *Daily Pro-Rata* recognition
   method and a recognition period.
2. In the *Milestones* tab, add milestones: a date and the cumulative
   amount to be recognized at the end of that date. Milestones outside
   the recognition period (start date included, end date excluded) are
   shown in red and ignored.
3. (Re)generate the schedule entries: the amount recognized at each
   month end follows the milestones.
