1. Create a performance obligation with the *Daily Pro-Rata* recognition
   method and a recognition period.
2. In the *Milestones* tab, add milestones: a date and the cumulative amount
   to be recognized at the end of that date. Milestones outside the
   recognition period (start date included, end date excluded) are shown in
   red and ignored.
3. (Re)generate the schedule entries: the amount recognized at each month
   end follows the milestones.
